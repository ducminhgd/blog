> "The world breaks everyone, and afterward, many are strong at the broken places."

— **Ernest Hemingway**, *A Farewell to Arms* (1929)

**Reflection:**

Hardship does not automatically make people stronger. Some experiences leave permanent damage. But recovery can create strengths, perspectives, and resilience that comfort alone rarely develops.

The important distinction: Being strong does not mean never being broken. It means learning how to continue after difficult experiences.


## 2026-11-26

> "It is not that we have a short time to live, but that we waste a lot of it."

— **Seneca**, *On the Shortness of Life*

**Reflection:** Time is not always the resource we lack. Sometimes the problem is how we spend it. Procrastination, distractions, and unnecessary commitments can consume years without producing anything meaningful.

The uncomfortable question: Are you spending your time, or merely passing it?

## 2026-11-25

> "Our doubts are traitors, and make us lose the good we oft might win, by fearing to attempt."

— **William Shakespeare**, *Measure for Measure*

**Reflection:** Fear of failure can become more damaging than failure itself. Sometimes the opportunity you lose by not trying is the greatest cost.
